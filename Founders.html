How we work

Each position is supported by extensive due diligence spanning industry dynamics, competitive positioning, financial fundamentals, management quality, and catalytic event timelines.

The intent is to build a concentrated portfolio of high-conviction positions and hold them through the realization of their underlying thesis, while a smaller tactical sleeve trades short-term inefficiencies in less-followed segments of the market.
